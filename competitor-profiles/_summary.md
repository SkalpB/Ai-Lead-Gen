## What they share (first principles)
1. **Craft = moat.** Hard-to-copy production makes the story credible; marketing amplifies, doesn't invent. [I, high]
2. **Earned > paid.** All three spend little visibly on performance ads; demand comes from press, prizes, celebrities, community. [I, medium — ad spend not public]
3. **Filter, don't funnel.** Each brand makes itself slightly "hard" (Loewe weirdness, péro whimsy, Kartik slow craft) to build belonging. [I, medium]

## Key differences
- **Loewe** runs a *culture engine* with LVMH capital — scale, IP collabs, meme-speed product response.
- **péro** runs a *slow compounding* model — wholesale trust over 15+ yrs, emotional design codes, almost no hype.
- **Kartik** runs a *founder-led global-first* model — US DTC + institutional validation before scale.

## Risks / challenges
- Loewe: post-Anderson continuity (McCollough & Hernandez from SS26) — brand culture was tied to one person. [O/I]
- péro: low visibility outside insiders; wholesale concentration risk. [I, medium]
- Kartik: US tariffs on Indian imports (flagged around FW26) hit a US-heavy DTC model; artisan capacity caps growth. [O/I]
